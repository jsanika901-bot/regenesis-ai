# Product Steering — RE:GENESIS AI

## Brand Identity
- **Name:** RE:GENESIS AI
- **Tagline:** Nothing Is Waste. Everything Has a Second Life.
- **Voice:** Precision industrial luxury meets ecological stewardship. Authoritative, transparent, grounded in real material science, never hyperbolic.

## Palette & Atmospheric Direction
- **Obsidian Black (`#060B09`):** Dominant background field representing precision hardware chassis and carbon neutrality.
- **Deep Forest Green (`#0A2218`):** Structural surface depth evoking regenerative forestry and anodized industrial enclosures.
- **Champagne Gold (`#D4AF37`):** Measured 10% accent reserved for primary actions, active navigation focus, and verified material recovery milestones.
- **Warm Ivory (`#F7F5EE`):** Crisp daylight-legible primary typography on dark surfaces.

## Product Truths & Guardrails
1. **Transparency Over Hype:** Every simulated hardware telemetry readout, thermal curve, and mechanical cycle must carry an explicit `Hardware Simulation Mode` label.
2. **Scientific Honesty:** Visual AI material recognition must explicitly state that optical classification cannot replace physical NIR spectroscopy or thermal calorimetry for chemical purity certification.
3. **Closed-Loop Continuity:** Scanning waste -> simulating raw feedstock recovery -> manufacturing a product -> updating inventory & CO2 ledger must operate as one unbroken, stateful data loop.
